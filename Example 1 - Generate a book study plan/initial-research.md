# Initial Research — The Unnatural Networker

Book: *The Unnatural Networker: How Anyone Can Succeed at Networking* by Charlie Lawson (2014).

## Author

- Charlie Lawson, UK speaker and trainer, founder of Unnatural Success.
- Long tenure as National Director of BNI UK & Ireland.
- Self-described "unnatural networker" — learned networking despite finding it uncomfortable.
- Follow-up book: *The Unnatural Promoter* (2021), on self-promotion without awkwardness.

## Premise

- Written for people who would rather avoid networking, including introverts and professionals short on confidence.
- Core claim: anyone can network effectively without pretending to be extroverted.
- Emphasis on purpose, human connection, and lasting relationships over pitching.

## Key themes

1. Don't sell at networking events — nobody attends looking to buy.
2. New contacts are not new relationships; measure relationships, not business cards.
3. Build relationships by learning people's needs, keeping in touch, adding value, and making introductions.
4. Be referable by telling stories, not listing what you do — feelings before and after are memorable and repeatable.
5. One-to-ones over coffee count as much as big events — a good entry point for introverts.
6. Later keynote framework: MAP — Message / Aim / Pursue (craft the message, target the right people, follow up).

## Reception

- A niche business book, used in professional-services training.
